# Powvy Habit campus manga theme

Scope: existing desktop/web workspace, Operate mode. Preserve routes, pagination, accessibility, data and sync. The user explicitly replaced the clean-white direction and asked for a stronger campus-manga treatment, a sleeping Power-like cat, night mode and the Powvy Habit name.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A usable manga notebook for everyday habits, not a white SaaS dashboard. The existing left rail and working panels stay in place.
OWN-WORLD: warm gray paper, charcoal ink outlines, deep red selected states, squared panel corners and restrained screentone gutters. Night mode reverses paper to graphite with warm readable text and muted salmon accents. No pure-white canvas.
STORY: Choose a tool from the left, enter or inspect records in place, switch lighting without changing data. The sleeping horned cat anchors the identity.
FIRST VIEWPORT: retain the 200px rail and two-column task workspace. A 48px cat and two-line Powvy Habit wordmark top the rail; compact day/night button sits beside account and language controls. Paper panels use ink frames and tab-like section titles; content remains quiet and readable.
FORM: user-pinned campus manga overrides direction seed 89bfc2cd. Code-led treatment of the existing operational layout; no structural redesign or comp needed. The signature interaction is a single instant day/night ink-and-paper inversion with remembered choice, respecting reduced motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Implementation: reuse CSS custom properties and a small pre-paint theme script; generate one original fan-inspired cat asset with the built-in image tool. Rename visible surfaces and downloads while retaining compatibility-sensitive storage, protocol and app ID. Publish a new preview without overwriting the original website.
